Curve: Strong, disciplined curve. (5) The mixed-recipe teach (L57) sits right next to the chain-order teach (L58), so two new ideas arrive back to back. (6) The Chapter 1 boss (L13, 87% casual) and the Chapter 3 boss (L39, 81%) are too soft next to L26, L52 and L64. (7) The late-third casual average is about 65% (L44-64 comes to 64.9%), right at the cap with no margin. The finale levels at 43-46% casual are on target.
