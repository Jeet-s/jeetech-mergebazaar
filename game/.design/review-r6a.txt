FAIL level_design: level-design review 7.7/10 (bar 8), 2 level(s) below 6/10: A well-crafted, readable set with a strong 'aha' on most levels and clear intent-to-board truth. It is let down by overuse of the Big Merge and jar tricks, one casual spike (L19), one unfair-feeling trap level (L59), a weak chain-order teach (L57), a misplaced banner teach (L32) and a redundant breather (L23). The finale trio (L62-64) is excellent. Fixing about 8 levels would lift the set to a clear 8+. Curve: Strong: one idea per level, readable intents, and the board geometry (cell-next-to-jar, ring reach, fifth-piece traps) is checkable. The numbers mostly meet the studio targets. L1-3 are 100/97/93% casual. No run of three or more easy levels appears after L5. The last-third casual average is about 64-65%, which is on the limit but acceptable. Skilled rates stay inside 50-95% except a few breathers.

Problems:
1. Big Merge is overused as the one aha. It appears in about 13 levels (L8, 9, 10, 12, 13, 20, 24, 25, 37, 39, 46, 55, 59). L12 and L46 are near-duplicates: the same four-corner Brass layout and the same "don't build the fifth Brass" trap. Jars are used the same way in about 13 levels ("merge beside it"). Late levels feel like variations of one trick.
2. L19 is a lone casual spike (62%) between 84% and 93% neighbours. It is a practise level inside the early-recipe stretch.
3. L59 (trap rate 0.33, casual 40%) hides a fatal early move: pairing the waiting keys. It has zero Spark slack, so it feels like guessing.
4. L57 teaches chain orders at 63% casual with no slack.
5. L31 and L33 (masala teach and twist) are split by an unrelated L32 (banner).
6. L23 is a 'breather' that mostly repeats L16 and L17 (Needle and a knotted generator).
7. Chapter bosses are inconsistent. L13 is barely harder than L12 (87% vs 85% casual). L26, L39, L52 and L64 escalate properly.
8. Boosters arrive in a sensible order (Fireworks L11, Needle L16, Sweep L47), but Sweep comes late. Several late levels give 6 Sparks while the optimal line uses 2 (L55, L47), which removes tension.
9. Breathers at L34, L42, L48 and L60 are well placed after spikes. L48 sits directly after a teach and is a second jar breather close to L42.
10. The finales (L62-64) are the best part of the set: they are dense, tell a story and test mastery. L64 is an excellent capstone.
