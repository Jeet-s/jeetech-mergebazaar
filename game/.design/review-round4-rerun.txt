Curve: Mechanic ordering is mostly excellent: merge, generator, orders, crates, Big Merge, Fireworks, thread, Needle, recipes, trunks and keys, 3-input recipes, jars, staged orders, Sweep, wrapped trunks, chain orders, then finales. Each mechanic gets a TEACH level, then PRACTISE, then a TWIST or COMBINE, and a boss closes every 13 levels (13, 26, 39, 52, 64). Problems:
1. Levels 2-6 are one long easy stretch (97, 91, 91, 99, 95). After level 5 the 90%-plus run continues into L6, and L14-15 and L27-28 are also easy pairs. All of them stay within the two-in-a-row rule.
2. Mixed recipes (L57) and chain orders (L58) arrive far too late, right before the finales. They are taught on top of a rule set that is already heavy, and they get no breathing room. Mixed recipes belong near L31-L38.
3. Sweep waits until L47. The Sweep, Needle and Fireworks teaches then feel isolated, and L47/L48 are near-duplicates.
4. The 'wasted Spark on a decoy' aha repeats too often: L2, L3, L4, L5, L7, L10, L31, L32 and L63. The 'don't make the fifth piece' aha is spent on L12, L37, L46 and L55, and the 'Big Merge bonus is an input' aha on L9, L24 and L46. The set needs fresh insights in the middle chapters.
5. The last-third casual average is about 65.3%, just over the 65% cap. Skilled averages about 90%, which is fine. Soften or tighten L46, L51 and L55, and make breathers L47, L48 and L60 more clearly easier than their neighbours. Late breathers such as L48 are not easier than the levels around them.
6. Spikes: L19 (63%) and L21 (58%) are unusually hard this early, between 80-90% neighbours. Chapter 3's boss (L39, 81%) is easier than chapter 2's (L26, 57%), so the boss ramp is not monotonic.
7. The doc promises a 7x9 board, but almost every level is 3x3 to 6x5. Only L64 uses the real board. Early boards are tiny, which is fine for teaching but gives little room to grow.
8. The doc promises a story beat every ~5 levels. Story levels exist at L5, 10, 20, 25, 30 and 35 only, with none at L15 or from L40 to L61.
