Nearly every level tests one named idea, the aha is true of the board, and decoys are honest. Solver proofs match the intents, trap rates are low, and L1-3 meet the win-rate targets. Weaknesses: (1) Big Merge timing is reused for the same aha in L12, L37 and L46, and L8-L10 are three close variants. (2) Many late levels have 0 spare Sparks and 12-17 optimal moves, which is long for casual players. (3) Chapter bosses are uneven: L13 (87% casual) and L39 (81%) are easier than their neighbours, while L26 and L52 are real tests. (4) Jars arrive late (L40) and Sweep arrives very late (L47), so the last third stacks many blocker combinations. (5) L62 (trap 0.2) and L63 (0.17) are the least fair: clutter plus one early tap can be fatal. (6) The last-third casual average is about 65.5%, just over the 65% cap, so ease one or two of the 43-60% levels (L58, L63). Against Royal Match or Flow Free the set has a strong teaching voice and theming, but little variety in board shape and few open, relaxed levels. Curve: L1-13 is a clean first chapter: merge, generator, orders, crates, Big Merge and Fireworks are each introduced alone, then combined in the L13 boss. But L8-10 are three Big Merge levels in a row, and L12-13 add more, so the chapter leans on one trick. L13 is too easy for a boss (87% casual, 97% skilled). Chapter 2 (L14-26) is well ordered: thread, Needle, recipes, then thread+recipe combinations. It has a breather at L23 and a real boss at L26. Chapter 3 (L27-39) introduces trunks, 3-input recipes and mixed recipes in good steps. L34 is a good breather. The L39 boss is overloaded (four systems) and still wins at 81% casual, so it neither teaches nor tests well. Chapter 4 (L40-52) introduces jars late, since thread and trunks have had 25 levels. Sweep appears as a teach only at L47. Boosters are staggered unevenly: Fireworks L11, Needle L16, Sweep L47. L40, L42 and L48 are three 100% levels, so L40-L48 reads as too gentle early and then spikes at L49-52 (67%, 60%, 53%, 53%). Chapter 5 (L53-64) is a run of chain orders, wrapped trunks and finales. Many of them have 0 spare Sparks and 11-17 optimal moves (L57, L58, L61, L64), so the late game turns into long, exact execution rather than insight. The L62-64 finales are on target in difficulty but L62 and L63 hide a fatal early tap. Overall: strong rhythm of teach, practise, twist, breather and boss. Fix the repeated Big Merge aha, move jar and Sweep teaches earlier, and add one Spark of slack to the zero-slack chain levels.
