FAIL level_design: level-design review 7/10 (bar 8), 1 level(s) below 6/10: A tightly authored 64-level set. Each level has a clear idea, a stated aha that the board actually supports, and a clean teach → practise → twist → combine rhythm across about 15 mechanics. The main problems are repetition (Big Merge 'wait for the fifth' appears five times, 'count the Sparks' about fifteen times), five levels with high trap rates (L55, L59, L39, L62, L13) that break the fairness rule, a late-third casual average of about 65.5% against the 65% cap, and boards that are almost all tiny (about 5x4). That leaves the design doc's 7x9 board, Bag and Sort unused until the finale. It is solid and more thoughtful than most, but it is not yet Royal Match or Flow Free polish. Curve: Strengths: the set is unusually well authored. Almost every level has a stated aha that matches its board, each new mechanic arrives alone with a readable teach (merge, crates, Big Merge, Fireworks, thread, Needle, recipes, trunks, 3-input recipes, jars, Sweep, staged orders, wrapped trunks, chain orders), and then comes a practise level, a twist and a combine. The chapter bosses are at L13, L26, L39 and L52, with finales at L62-64. Rest levels (L23, L34, L42, L48) follow harder stretches. Casual win rates fall roughly 100 → 85 → 70 → 55, and the two-easy-in-a-row cap is respected. Weaknesses: 1) The last-third casual average is about 65.5%, just over the 65% cap. L48 (100%), L47 (85%), L57 (87%), L43 (82%) and L55 (80%) are the easy ones to tighten. 2) The trap rates on L55 (0.5), L59 (0.33), L39 (0.25), L62 (0.2) and L13 (0.17) break the 'no single hidden fatal move' rule. The late 'hard' levels are mostly hard because of one irreversible mistake, not because of depth. 3) Repetition. 'Don't merge before the fifth piece arrives' recurs in L12, L37, L46, L55 and L59. 'Count your Sparks exactly' is the aha in about 15 levels, and L5, L6 and L7 are close cousins. Big Merge appears in about 14 levels. 4) Thread takes up L14-L26 and jar and trunk levels run L27-L56, so the middle is long and one-note per stretch. Boosters Fireworks, Needle and Sweep are each taught once and barely reused, and the finales never test thread or boosters. 5) Board scale does not match the design doc. The doc promises a 7x9 board with Bag, Sort and tidy-board scoring, but nearly every level fits in about 5x4 and the 7x9 board is used once at the end. Space pressure, Bag and Sort are never exercised, so the real merge-game feel (clutter management) is missing until L62. Recommendation: cut or merge the duplicate Big Merge 'wait for the fifth' levels, soften the trap cells with a spare Spark or an Undo-safe recovery, and add 3-4 bigger-board levels in L30-L55 that use Bag, Sort and clutter.
