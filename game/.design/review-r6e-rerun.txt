FAIL level_design: level-design review 7.6/10 (bar 8), 0 level(s) below 6/10: Strong, literate puzzle set. Nearly every level has a stated insight and the board backs it up. The count-the-pieces and read-the-queue ahas beat most of the genre, and the 13-level chapter rhythm is clean (bosses at 13, 26, 39, 52, then a 3-level finale). Mechanics are layered well: merge, Big Merge, thread, recipes, trunks, jars, staged orders, chain orders. Five problems keep it below top tier. (1) It plays as a tight Spark-budget puzzle rather than a merge game: almost all boards are 3x3 to 5x4, and the 7x9 board, Bag, Sort and clutter management only appear in L62 and L64. L62 then punishes board management with trap rate 0.2. (2) Near-duplicates: L34 and L48 are the same four-Brass rangoli round a locked centre; L27 and L28 share the chilli-trunk decoy; L24 and L25 are the same Big-Merge-ring-frees-the-dye idea; L12 and L46 are the same don't-make-the-fifth twist; decoy generators carry too many levels (L2, L3, L7, L31, L63). (3) Boss difficulty is inconsistent: L13 (87% casual) and L39 (81% casual, 100% skilled) are easier than the levels before them, while L26 (57%) spikes against neighbours at 71% and 93%. (4) There is no breather from L49 to L61. The last breather is L48, then about 14 levels sit at 53-73% casual with several zero-spare budgets. The last-third casual average is about 65%, right at the limit. (5) Boosters and story beats are thin. Fireworks appear only in L11 and L13, Needle in L16 and L26, and Sweep arrives at L47. Story beats run L5, 10, 20, 25, 30, 35 and then jump to L60, against the doc's one-per-5-levels promise. Curve: Levels 1-13 ramp well: merge, a decoy generator, crates, queue-reading, Big Merge, then a boss. Casual wins sit at 77-100%. L8 (100%) and L11 (95%) are the only easy spikes, so the 'no more than 2 easy in a row' rule holds. Chapter 2 (L14-26) introduces thread, Needle and recipes, then combines them at L21-L26. Two problems here. L24 and L25 are back-to-back variants of the same idea. The L26 boss spikes to 57% after 71% and is followed by 93% at L27. Chapter 3 (L27-39) covers trunks, 3-input recipes and trunk-plus-Big-Merge combinations. It is solid, but the trunk decoy is reused in L27/L28, L32 is a hard 67% spike straight after the 95% L31, and the L39 boss is easier than L38. Chapter 4 (L40-52) brings jars, staged orders and Sweep. Sweep is taught very late (L47) and the stage/jar combos get steadily harder. L49-L52 form a wall at 53-67% casual after L48's breather. Chapter 5 (L53-64) is wrapped trunks, chain orders and the finale. It has no breather between L48 and the finale, L57 teaches chain orders at 63% casual with zero spare, and the last third averages about 65% casual (at the limit). Three finale levels in a row (L62 at 61%, L63 at 46%, L64 at 43%) are a steep climax. L62's trap rate of 0.2 and L63's 0.17 mean a hidden fatal tap in the finale, which should be telegraphed or forgiven. Placement fixes: add a breather around L57-58 and another before L62; add story beats at roughly L40, L45, L50 and L55; give the Fireworks, Needle and Sweep one more appearance each in a different context; and play at least one full-board level before L62.
