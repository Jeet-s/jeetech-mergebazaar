**Rendering**
- **Root cause:** the story was a see-through sheet over the board, so mid-fade frames showed its text, its buttons and the toolbar ghosted together.

- **Popups:** cards now turn opaque within the first third of their pop-in.
- **Other story card:** the speech bubble no longer runs off the edge in the replay from the Story book.
- **Board pieces:** art is now drawn inset in its tile, and the corner badges (next item, Spark cost, layer counts) sit inside the tile.

**Game feel**
- **Order filled:** tapping the order card skipped the whole effect, because the card's position was only ever measured during a drag. The piece now flies to the card, bursts, and a golden flare blooms behind the card.
- **Merges:** the piece makes a short hop into its partner, then the result squashes, overshoots and flashes.
- **Win:** the board celebrates first (screen pulse, a diya rising at the board's centre with turning rays, sparkles across the tiles, a frame pulse). The result card follows about 1.7 s later.
- **Win card:** stars stamp in one by one, then the Lamps row springs up and counts from 0 (no static "+0"), then the heirloom card slides in, and Next keeps breathing.
- **Tutorial:** on levels 1–2 the board dims around the tile the hand points at.
- **Home after a win:** chapter progress fills in from where it was, and a pulsing "Restore! N★" button appears once there are enough stars.
