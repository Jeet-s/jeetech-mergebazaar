**Root causes and fixes**
- **known_pitfalls:** the screen container in `App.tsx` is now a stable view that eases its opacity instead of a keyed view with `entering`/`exiting`. The banner now clears the gesture bar. All the other keyed or `entering` views are gone. Every flagged plain `Pressable` now squashes on touch-down, and the confetti and level-map canvases no longer redraw on every move.
- **Petals over the win title:** the cause was the rotating sparkle image behind the stars. It was larger than its box, sat over the subtitle, and its art includes petals. It is now clipped to the stars band, and the win confetti draws behind the card. Checked in a screenshot.
- **Stale coach text:** once the order is filled the line changes to "Order filled — Dadi's doorstep glows!". The "Order filled!" text that floated over the order icon is gone; the coach line says it instead.
- **Slow tap response:**
  - Board pieces now squash and light up on touch-down without waiting for a React re-render.
  - All buttons respond on touch-down with sound and haptics.
  - The tutorial hand now draws a dotted drag path with a target ring.
  - The Bag label no longer flips to "Store".
  - The home screen has twinkling lamps, a glowing doorway and a shine across the Play button.
  - The story card is about half as tall, so the Lamp Shelf stays visible.
- **Merge preview (was missing):** holding a piece for about 0.4 s shows a card above the board with the piece, its count on the board, the result (and the Big Merge bonus) and one line of text. Holding a stall shows what it makes next and its cost; a hold never spends a Spark. Level 1 now teaches the hold as a step. Checked in screenshots.
- **Rewarded Spark refill (was missing):**
  - The Sparks bar now has a "+" and opens a "More Sparks" popup with "+3 Sparks (watch ad)", once per attempt.
  - The same button appears on the "Out of moves" card when Sparks ran out.
  - It also opens when you tap a stall without enough Sparks.
  - If no ad loads, the Sparks are granted anyway. That is a judgement call, so the button never dead-ends.
  - I tested it from 0/6 Sparks back to 3/6 in the preview.
