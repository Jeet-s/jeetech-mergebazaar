**Factory issues (not patched)**
1. **Silent-move check:** `lib/qa/device.ts` crops 30% of the screen width around the touch, which is smaller than one cell on 4–5 column boards. It should crop at least a full cell, or compare the whole board.
2. **Level review is noisy and fights the pacing gate:** the score swings by up to 0.8 between nearly identical sets, and the review reversed its own advice between runs on L8, L28, L29 and L55. Averaging several runs would help.
3. **Bots:** they can't plan merges beside jars or knots on tight budgets, so late budgets have to go up, and the review then calls them loose. One move of look-ahead would help.
4. **No privacy URL or support email:** neither `game.config.json` nor the app config has one, so the in-app privacy link the GDD asks for can't be shown.
5. **Cloud save and support tickets:** the GDD asks for sign-in cloud save and support tickets, which the no-accounts, no-network rule forbids. The offline backup code is the substitute, so the tester's "Account sign-in" check may still flag it.
