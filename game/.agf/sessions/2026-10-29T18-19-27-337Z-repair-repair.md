I've fixed all three blocking checks (render_defects, game_feel, feature_tour). `npx tsc --noEmit`, `npm run pitfalls` (0 errors), `npm run validate` (64/64 solvable), `npm run playtest`, `npm run levels` and `level-review.ts` (8/10, exit 0) all pass. I checked the level 1 and level 3 screens in a web build of the real code at 360×740. That is not a device; the factory's QA round still needs to confirm it on Android.

**Why it kept failing.** The last repair only moved the tutorial hand around; this time I changed how it behaves. The tour failure had a separate cause: the game had no Android Back handling at all, so the tester's Back press closed the app. Boosters were also hidden until level 11, which the tour never reaches.

**Rendering**
- **Tutorial hand:** it no longer rests on the board. Each loop it moves in, presses, leaves, and is gone for about a third of the loop; the target tile keeps glowing while it's away. For "deliver" moves it only sets off toward the order card and fades out, so it never crosses the hint banner or sits on the card's count. Taps press slightly above the tile's centre.
- **Spark bar:** shows "3 Sparks" instead of current/max, so "3/1" can't appear. After a refill it measures against the most Sparks held this attempt.
- **"+3 Sparks!" text:** now rises over the middle of the board, never over the top bar. Three Spark icons fly from there into the bar. The floating text has a taller line and side padding so glyphs aren't clipped.
- **Order card:** the item icon sits fully inside the card, and the 0/1 count (or tick) now sits below the card face instead of on top of the icon. Clear-goal cards use the same layout.
- **Stalls:** the corner badges are gone. Each stall has a price tag along the bottom of its tile (⚡ cost › next item), and the stall art is smaller and raised to make room.

**Game feel**
- **Level 1 now has 3 Sparks** (the job needs 1), so a curious extra tap can't hit "Not enough Sparks" and an ad offer. I removed the "press and hold to preview" step from the level 1 tutorial; it contradicted the drag the hand was showing. `docs/LEVELS.md` is updated.
- **Stall tap:** a Spark flies from the bar into the stall, which squashes harder and gets a ring-and-sparkle burst.
- **Tap you can't pay for:** the Spark bar shakes and flashes red, the "+" pulses three times, and the stall's price tag flashes red.
- **Ready order card:** bobs gently while it glows.
- **Win card:** confetti now comes in four waves over about 4 seconds from alternating sides, and earned stars keep twinkling after they land.
- **Brass Diya:** recoloured to bright gold so it reads clearly against the red Clay Diya.

**Feature tour**
- **Back button:** on a level it backs out of an armed booster, open Bag or popup first, then goes Home like the Home button. On the level map and restore screens it returns Home. Only Back on Home exits the app.
- **Boosters:** new players get 1 Fireworks and 1 Needle, and the booster tray shows from level 2. A booster stays in the tray for the rest of the attempt after it's used up, with "+" to buy more. Fireworks on tiles with nothing locked still bursts and says so. On level 3 I used Fireworks for real in the web build and the `booster_used` event fired. The bottom buttons shrink slightly on narrow phones so six fit.

**Not verified:** I couldn't trigger the out-of-Sparks feedback or the refill fly-in in the web build. Taps there registered as long-presses and ads are stubbed, so those two paths are checked by reading the code only.

**Not fixed:** the level reviewer still notes a few non-blocking design issues: L13 is easy for a boss, L24 and L25 are near-duplicates, and there's no breather around L60.
